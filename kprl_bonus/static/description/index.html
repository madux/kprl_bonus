KPRL Bonus Management

Comprehensive Bonus & Commission Engine with Governance

Full bonus lifecycle management for KPRL, covering parameter configuration, pool computation, KPI scorecard, gate management, staff payout, sales commissions, deferred ledger, governance log, and CEO/superadmin control panel.

Key Features

  • Control Panel — CEO/Superadmin configures all bonus parameters with governance limits & approval workflow
  • Bonus Engine — Computes effective pool, gate status, KPI scorecard, proration & payout
  • Employee Master — Grade, sub-pool, tenure, points & token rate tracking
  • Performance Inputs — Appraisal scores, sales targets & achievement per period
  • Staff Payouts — Gross pay, deferred amounts, net immediate with full multiplier breakdown
  • Sales Commission — Territory/RSM targets, commission rates, accelerators & caps
  • Deferred Ledger — Deferred bonus tracking with vesting schedules
  • Governance — Malus, clawback, risk register & audit trail
  • Reference Tables — Grades, tenure multipliers, dept KPI multipliers, subpool limits, role parameters
  • Product Pool — SKU-level revenue & bonus pool contribution tracking
